Getting started

In the app, tap ⓘ → How to use for a step-by-step tutorial. In short:

  1. Import a screenshot of an airport chart that shows its scale bar (Photos, Files or Paste).
  2. Calibrate: check the two points the app places on the scale bar, adjust them if needed and press Use. No scale bar found? Place two points on any known distance and enter it.
  3. Measure: place two points, for example the threshold and an exit. Zoom in for a smaller ±.

Common questions

Why does a distance show a ± range?
Screenshots, scale bars and finger placement all have small errors. The app shows the 95% range instead of false precision. Zooming in before placing points and using a long reference both make it smaller.

Why is measuring blocked?
The chart contains a “NOT TO SCALE” marking. If it only applies to an inset, not the main chart, you can choose Measure anyway; the warning stays visible.

The scale bar was not detected.
Use a sharper or larger screenshot, or calibrate manually on the scale bar or a printed runway length.

Are my measurements saved?
Only while the chart is open. They are discarded when you import another chart or close the app.
