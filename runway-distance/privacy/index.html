Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Runway Distance is an iPad app that measures distances on airport chart screenshots.

Summary

Runway Distance collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Images you import

Chart images you import from Photos, Files or the clipboard are processed on your iPad only. Text recognition (for example, finding the scale bar or “not to scale” markings) uses Apple’s on-device frameworks. Images are never uploaded, shared or sent to the developer or any third party.

The app only reads the images you choose. It does not access the rest of your photo library.

Data stored on your device

The app stores nothing permanently. Measurements are kept in memory while a chart is open and are discarded when you import another chart or close the app.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet. The “Privacy policy” and “Support” buttons in the app’s About screen open this website in Safari.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party resources. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and operation, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email

If you email the developer, your email address and message are used only to answer you and are not shared with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
